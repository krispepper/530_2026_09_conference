User testing:
1. Publish an open conference with one seat, a future event date,
   and a registration deadline still in the future.
2. Register participant u3 to fill the seat.
3. As participant u4, join the waiting list.
4. Leave the waiting list and check the confirmation, then join again.
5. As u3, unregister.
6. As u4, confirm the conference appears in My conferences as Registered
   and the waiting-list entry is gone.

Use verified UAT accounts if u3 and u4 are unavailable.
