
Known UAT test data
-------------------
The seeded UAT data includes these users:

       u1 - organizer
       u2 - administrator and owner of Spring Technology Research Forum
       u3 - participant
       u4 - participant invited to the restricted workshop

The login form uses the user ID. Obtain the corresponding UAT password
securely from the instructor or UAT administrator before recording the test.
Do not assume that a local development password is valid in UAT.

Seeded conferences:

       c1 - Adelphi Software Engineering Conference
            Open registration, capacity 100, owned by u1
       c2 - Spring Technology Research Forum
            Open registration, capacity 75, owned by u2
       c3 - Invited Leadership Workshop
            Restricted registration, capacity 25, owned by u1

Sprint 3 test plan
------------------

1. Registration receipt

   a. Log in as u3 or another registered participant.
   b. Open View conferences and select an available conference.
   c. Select Register and confirm that the conference shows the registered
      state.
   d. Select Download receipt.
   e. Confirm that a receipt file downloads successfully.
   f. Open the file and verify that it contains:
         - participant name
         - unique registration number
         - submission date
         - selected conference/session
         - current session details, including date/time and location
   g. Open a conference for which the user has no registration and attempt
      the receipt URL. Confirm that the response says:
         No registration was found.
   h. Log out and attempt to open a receipt URL. Confirm that the application
      redirects to the login page and does not display registration data.

2. Organizer registration rules

   a. Log in as u1.
   b. Confirm that u1's own conferences are not shown in View conferences.
   c. Confirm that conferences owned by another user can be viewed and
      registered for when capacity is available.
   d. Open u1's own conference directly, if needed, and confirm that the page
      says the organizer cannot register for their own conference.
   e. Log in as u2 and repeat the ownership check for c2. The administrator
      owner must not be able to register for c2, but may register for a
      conference owned by another user if the conference permits it.

3. Participant CSV authorization

   a. Log in as the organizer who owns a conference, such as u1 for c1 or c3.
   b. Open the owned conference page.
   c. Confirm that Download participant CSV is visible.
   d. Download the CSV and verify that it contains participant registration
      information.
   e. Log in as u3 or another participant and open a conference page.
   f. Confirm that Download participant CSV is not visible.
   g. Log in as an organizer who does not own the conference and confirm that
      the option is not visible.
   h. Direct access to the CSV URL must also be rejected for non-owners.

4. Navigation and session behavior

   a. While logged out, open Home. Confirm that Home shows Log in and Create
      new user, but the top toolbar does not show View conferences or Log in.
   b. Log in successfully.
   c. Confirm that the top toolbar shows the signed-in user and Log out.
   d. Confirm that View conferences and My Conferences are available.
   e. Select Home. Confirm that the session remains active, the user is still
      shown as signed in, and Log out remains available.
   f. Select Log out. Confirm that the session is cleared.
   g. Confirm that the signed-out toolbar no longer shows View conferences or
      Log out.
